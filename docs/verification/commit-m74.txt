Gate 85 — M74 BottomSheet (coverage 90 → 91)

The BottomSheet is the sheet that comes up from the bottom of the
screen, and it comes in the shapes a sheet can be.

The port carries upstream's shape. The sheet is a native `<dialog>`, so
the page behind it is inert while it is open, Escape closes it, and the
platform handles the focus. The sheet never opens or closes itself:
`is_open` and `on_open_change` are the caller's, which is the only way
a sheet can be told to stay put. The purpose decides how it may be
dismissed — an ordinary sheet goes away on a scrim click, a form sheet
keeps what was typed into it, and a required sheet refuses every
implicit dismissal and is an `alertdialog` because it has to be
answered. A sheet with stops settles at one of them when the handle is
dragged, and an inline sheet is ordinary markup written into the page,
with no top layer and no scrim.

## The gate found

- The worker's section was not the house's shape at all: it drew its
  own headings and its own wrappers, so it produced no
  `data-showcase-component` and no `data-example` markers, and the
  browser gate could not see it. The section is now the house's
  `section`/`ex` pair, and the examples are graphs mapped to nodes
  before they are wrapped.
- `Bonsai.state'` hands back an update-style setter, and the setter is
  a tree, not a function: `set (fun _ -> false)`, reached through
  `Bonsai.map` where it is used.
- A labelled argument cannot shadow the function of the same name: the
  helper's `~heading` and `~note` parameters hid the `heading` and
  `note` functions inside the helper's own body. They are `~title` and
  `~copy` now.
- `Vdom.Attr.on_click` takes a mouse event, not a unit.

## The gate changed

- The component case no longer asserts that the dialog carries the
  `open` attribute: jsdom has no `showModal`, so the open state is
  checked in the real browser instead, where the sheet is a genuine
  `:modal` dialog.
- The real-browser checks cover the four dismissal rules: the sheet
  opens as a modal that names itself, Escape closes it, and a sheet
  that has to be answered is an `alertdialog` while an ordinary one is
  a plain `dialog`.
