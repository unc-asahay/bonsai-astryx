Gate 91 — M80 DateTimeInput (coverage 96 → 97)

The date time input is a date field, a time field, and a calendar to pick
the date from.

The field owns the draft text of the date and of the time, and whether
the calendar is open; the application owns the committed reading. The
reading has no zone and no offset: it is a calendar date and a time of
day, which is the same information upstream's ISO string carries. The
hour format chooses between the twelve hour clock, which is the default,
and the twenty-four hour clock, and the minute increment is the step the
time field's arrow keys move by.

The worker's 813-line implementation compiled against the house without
repair, which is the first time that has happened. It needed a CSS part
and a showcase section, and one fix: the example that shows the
twenty-four hour clock had no reading, so the clock had nothing to show
and the assertion had nothing to check. An afternoon reading on a
twenty-four hour clock is the point of the example, so it now carries
one.
