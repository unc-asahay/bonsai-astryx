Gate 82 — M71 Calendar (coverage 87 → 88)

The Calendar is the month grid: one month or two, the days and their
states, the range pick, the keyboard walk over the grid, and the live
announcement of what a pick did.

The port carries upstream's shape: the grid is a `role="grid"` with the
month as its accessible name, every day is a `role="gridcell"` holding a
button that names its full date ("Thursday, October 1, 2026"), a range
grid is `aria-multiselectable`, and a day that cannot be picked says so
with `aria-disabled` rather than vanishing from the keyboard order.

Upstream's `PlainDate` is ported as `src/plain_date.ml` — the ISO dates,
the month arithmetic, the day and week numbers, the locale-aware month
names — and it is the foundation the date inputs build on.

## The gate found

- `external` is a reserved word in OCaml; the worker's `let external =`
  did not parse.
- The worker's `and render` sat after a `;;`, orphaned from its `let`;
  the binding is now `let rec view … and render …`.
- The month grid was missing a closing `]`.
- `node##focus` needs the js_of_ocaml preprocessor, which the library
  does not use; the focus call is now `Js.Unsafe.meth_call`.
- The `model` record type was never written; the worker's own uses said
  what it must hold — the selection, the month in view, the focused day,
  the Tab stops, the range's anchor, the hovered day, the announcement.
- `min_range_span` is an `int`, not an option.
- `List.assoc_opt` does not exist in this Core; it is `List.Assoc.find`.
- `List.nth` returns an option here.
- The attrs are lists: `Common.Attr.aria` returns a list and has to be
  spliced with `@`.
- `Bonsai.state'` hands back an update-style setter — the setter takes
  the next state from the current one, so it is `set (fun _ -> next)`.

## The gate changed

A day that cannot be picked is marked `aria-disabled` instead of taking
the native `disabled` attribute, so it keeps its place in the keyboard
order. Upstream distinguishes the two cases — a constraint-rejected day
is natively disabled, a day outside the range span is only aria-disabled
— and the house's `day_state` does not separate them, so the port takes
the gentler of the two and says so in the interface.
